# Credits

The conjecture is posed in R. Lyons, *Determinantal probability: basic properties and conjectures*, Proc. Int.
Congress Math. 2014, vol. IV, 137-161. The case of Z^d is due to R. Lyons and J. E. Steif, *Stationary determinantal
processes: phase multiplicity, Bernoullicity, entropy, and domination*, Duke Math. J. 120 (2003), 515-575.

The paper rests on the following work.

- H. Li and A. Thom, *Entropy, determinants, and L^2-torsion*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 27 (2014): the approximation
  theorem for the Fuglede-Kadison determinant used for the amenable case.
- L. E. Labuschagne, *A noncommutative Szego theorem for subdiagonal subalgebras of von Neumann algebras*, Proc. Amer.
  Math. Soc. 133 (2005), and D. P. Blecher and L. E. Labuschagne, *Applications of the Fuglede-Kadison determinant:
  Szego's theorem and outers for noncommutative H^p*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 360 (2008): the Jensen inequality for subdiagonal algebras used for ordered
  groups. W. Arveson, *Analyticity in operator algebras*, Amer. J. Math. 89 (1967): subdiagonal algebras.
- T. Austin, *Entropy and determinants for unitary representations*, arXiv:2412.13751: the determinant identity that
  the ordered case uses is a case of his Theorem B.
- R. Lyons, *Determinantal probability measures*, Publ. Math. IHES 98 (2003): the conditioning of determinantal
  measures (Prop. 8.3) and the law of the complement. Theorem 2.9 of the conjecture paper gives domination of the
  product measure of density p whenever pI <= K, and R. Lyons and A. Thom, *Invariant coupling of determinantal
  measures on sofic groups*, Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems 36 (2016), show that on finitely generated sofic groups such
  a monotone coupling can be chosen invariant.
- H. Kesten, *Symmetric random walks on groups*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 92 (1959): the spectral criterion for
  amenability used for the nonamenable case.
- G. Ray and Y. Spinka, *Characterizations of amenability through stochastic domination and finitary codings*, Trans.
  Amer. Math. Soc. (2026): a related characterization of amenability through stochastic domination.

The full list of references is in the paper.
